Uprint is a cloud-based campus printing solution designed to make student printing convenient, queue-free, cashless, and secure — while working with your university's existing printer infrastructure.

Built for Student & Campus Printing

Students can print from their phones or computers and collect their documents from a nearby Uprint printer. The process is simple and self-service, so students don't need to use shared computers or wait for staff assistance.

How Uprint Helps Universities

Student-Friendly Printing

Students can print directly from their own phones or computers, without depending on shared lab computers to get a document out.

Queue-Free & Self-Service

Students submit print jobs remotely and collect them when ready, reducing the need for manual assistance at the printer.

Works With Existing Printers

Uprint can work with your existing online and offline printers, with minimal changes to your current infrastructure.

Centralized Printing Management

The university can monitor printers, page usage, users, and printing activity from a centralized dashboard.

Secure File Handling

Uploaded documents are automatically deleted after printing, reducing the risk of student documents remaining on shared systems.

Cashless Printing

Uprint supports printing credits or per-page payment, allowing universities to provide cashless printing without manual cash handling.

Less Maintenance. More Control.

Faster for students. Easier for the university. A better campus printing experience.
