Dear Sir,

We are grateful for the opportunity to present our proposal for UPrint, our campus printing solution, to your esteemed institution, Premier University. UPrint is a cloud-based printing platform designed to make student printing queue-free, cashless, secure, and convenient, while working with your existing printing infrastructure.

How Uprint Works

Students can submit documents directly from their mobile phones or computers and collect their printed documents from a dedicated UPrint kiosk with a simple QR code scan. The solution can benefit both the university and its students by reducing printing time, operational workload, and infrastructure-related costs.

Two Plans for Premier University

Plan A · Hosted on UPrint's Server

1 Month Free Trial (Up to 1 Device)

Premier University can experience UPrint on campus for one full month at no cost on up to 1 device. No monthly charge is required during the trial period, so you can evaluate the platform with zero risk.

Plan A Pricing

Monthly & Yearly Fees (per device)

After the free trial, UPrint is charged at BDT 1,000 per device per month.

Period Per Device
Month 1 (Free Trial) BDT 0
Monthly fee (after trial) BDT 1,000
First year (11 paid months) BDT 11,000
Every following year (12 months) BDT 12,000

Total yearly cost = number of devices × the per-device amount above.

UPrint Device Cost

Each UPrint device is a one-time purchase, with no recurring hardware charge.

One-time cost: BDT 5,000 per device

Device Warranty

15-day money-back warranty: if you are not satisfied with a UPrint device, you can return it within 15 days of delivery for a refund.

1-year replacement warranty: if a device has any software-related issue, we will provide a new one free of cost.

Please note: the warranty becomes void in case of physical damage or voltage fluctuation issues, and a parts damage charge will apply.
